ADAPTIVE HD CALL QUALITY

- Requests Full HD (1920x1080) camera video at up to 30 fps when supported.
- Falls back safely to HD (1280x720) on devices without Full HD capture.

- Uses a detail-oriented video track hint for clearer faces and edges.
- Configures WebRTC for up to 4 Mbps on desktop and 3 Mbps on mobile.
- Reduces bitrate adaptively on 3G, 2G, and weak connections.
- Prefers maintaining resolution when bandwidth becomes constrained.
- Reapplies quality settings after camera switching, screen sharing, and connection changes.
- Enables echo cancellation, noise suppression, and automatic microphone gain.
- Preserves the iPhone remote-filter compatibility from Build 6807.
