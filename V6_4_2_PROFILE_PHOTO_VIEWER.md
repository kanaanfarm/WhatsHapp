# ConnectChat Pro Enterprise v6.4.2

## Large profile photo viewer

- Click or press Enter on the large profile avatar to open the original photo.
- Zoom from 50% to 400% using the controls or mouse wheel.
- Reset zoom to 100%.
- Close with the close button, backdrop, or Escape key.
- Responsive full-screen presentation on desktop and mobile.
- Profile-photo editing remains restricted to the account owner.
